# Current Account Terms and Conditions

## 1. Eligibility

This account is available to individuals aged 18 or older who hold a valid Bahraini CPR or a valid residence permit. A minimum opening deposit of 50 BHD is required. Joint accounts require both holders to complete separate identity verification.

## 2. Minimum Balance

Accounts must maintain a minimum balance of 100 BHD at all times. If the balance falls below this threshold for more than 30 consecutive days, a monthly maintenance fee of 5 BHD will be applied until the balance is restored.

## 3. Statements

Monthly statements are issued electronically by default. Paper statements can be requested and are subject to a 2 BHD fee per statement. Statements are retained and accessible for 7 years from the date of issue, in line with regulatory record-keeping requirements.

## 4. Account Closure

Customers may close their account at any time by visiting a branch or submitting a request through the mobile app. Accounts with a negative balance cannot be closed until the outstanding amount is settled. Closure requests are processed within 3 business days.

## 5. Dormancy

An account is classified as dormant if there has been no customer-initiated transaction for 12 consecutive months. Dormant accounts continue to accrue any applicable fees but will not be closed automatically. Reactivation requires the customer to visit a branch with valid identification.
