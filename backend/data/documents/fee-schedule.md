# Fee Schedule

## 1. Account Fees

The standard current account carries no monthly fee provided the minimum balance of 100 BHD is maintained. Falling below this threshold triggers a 5 BHD monthly maintenance fee, charged on the last business day of the month.

## 2. Card Fees

Debit card issuance is free for the first card. A replacement card, whether lost, stolen, or damaged, costs 5 BHD. Expedited replacement, delivered within 24 hours, costs an additional 10 BHD on top of the standard replacement fee.

## 3. Transfer Fees

Transfers between accounts at the same bank are free of charge. Outgoing transfers to other local banks within Bahrain cost 1 BHD per transfer. International wire transfers cost 15 BHD plus any correspondent bank charges, which are not controlled by the bank and may vary.

## 4. Foreign Currency Fees

Transactions made in a currency other than BHD are converted at the prevailing exchange rate on the day of settlement, plus a 2.5% foreign transaction fee. This fee applies to both point-of-sale purchases and ATM withdrawals made abroad.

## 5. Overdraft Fees

Unauthorized overdrafts are charged at 8 BHD per occurrence, in addition to interest on the negative balance calculated daily at an annual rate of 12%. Authorized overdraft facilities, arranged in advance, carry a lower annual rate of 7% and no per-occurrence fee.
