# Complaints Procedure

## 1. How to File a Complaint

Complaints can be submitted through the mobile app, by calling customer service, by email, or in person at any branch. Each complaint is assigned a reference number, which the customer receives immediately upon submission for tracking purposes.

## 2. Response Timeline

The bank acknowledges every complaint within 2 business days. A full response, including the outcome and any corrective action, is provided within 14 business days. Complex complaints involving third parties may take up to 30 business days, with the customer notified of the extension.

## 3. Escalation

If a customer is not satisfied with the resolution, they may request escalation to a senior complaints officer within 10 business days of receiving the response. Escalated complaints are reviewed independently of the original case handler.

## 4. External Referral

If a complaint remains unresolved after escalation, the customer may refer the matter to the Central Bank of Bahrain's Consumer Protection Unit. The bank is required to inform customers of this right in its final response letter.

## 5. Compensation

Where a complaint is upheld and the customer has incurred a direct financial loss due to a bank error, compensation is issued directly to the account within 5 business days of the final decision. Compensation for non-financial inconvenience is assessed on a case-by-case basis.
