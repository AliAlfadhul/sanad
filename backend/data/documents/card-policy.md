# Card Usage Policy

## 1. Activation

New debit cards must be activated within 90 days of issuance, either through the mobile app, at an ATM, or by calling customer service. Cards not activated within this period are automatically deactivated and must be reissued, subject to the standard replacement fee.

## 2. Daily Limits

The default daily withdrawal limit is 500 BHD at ATMs and 1000 BHD for point-of-sale purchases. Customers may request a temporary or permanent increase to these limits through the mobile app, subject to a review of account activity and standing.

## 3. Card Freezing

Customers can freeze and unfreeze their card instantly through the mobile app without contacting customer service. A frozen card blocks all new transactions but does not cancel pending or recurring transactions that were authorized before the freeze was applied.

## 4. Lost or Stolen Cards

A lost or stolen card must be reported immediately through the mobile app or the 24-hour customer service line. Once reported, the card is permanently blocked and cannot be reactivated. A replacement card is issued automatically unless the customer requests otherwise.

## 5. International Use

Cards are enabled for international use by default unless the customer has opted out through the mobile app. Enabling international use also enables online purchases from foreign merchants, which are otherwise blocked as a fraud prevention measure.
